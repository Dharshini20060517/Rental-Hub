    42→    42→## Phase 2: Booking System & Rental Lifecycle — COMPLETE
    43→    43→
    44→    44→### New data model
    45→    45→
    46→    46→Six new Prisma models added to `prisma/schema.prisma`:
    47→    47→
    48→    48→- **`Booking`** — `equipmentId`, `customerId`, `ownerId`, `status`, `previousStatus`, `startDate`,
    49→    49→  `endDate`, `days`, `baseAmount`, `adjustments` (JSON), `depositAmount`, `commissionRate` (10%),
    50→    50→  `commissionAmount`, `totalAmount`, `cancellationPolicy` (`flexible`/`moderate`/`strict`),
    51→    51→  `refundAmount`, `holdExpiresAt`, `confirmedAt`, `pickedUpAt`, `returnedAt`, `inspectedAt`,
    52→    52→  `completedAt`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelReason`, `pickupChecklist`/`returnChecklist`/`pickupPhotos`/
    53→    53→  `returnPhotos` (JSON), `idempotencyKey`. Compound index on `{equipmentId, startDate, endDate,
    54→    54→  status}` for concurrency, secondary indexes on `customerId+status`, `ownerId+status`,
    55→    55→  `status`, `holdExpiresAt`, and `@@unique([idempotencyKey])`.
    56→    56→- **`BookingStatusHistory`** — append-only audit trail (`fromStatus`, `toStatus`, `actorId`,
    57→    57→  `note`).
    58→    58→- **`Payment`** — `bookingId`, `userId`, `type` (`hold`/`release`/`refund`/`charge`/`commission`),
    59→    59→  `amount`, `status` (`pending`/`completed`/`failed`/`reversed`), `reference` (simulated gateway
    60→    60→  ref), `idempotencyKey`.
    61→    61→- **`BookingMessage`** — chat between customer and owner on a booking (`senderId`, `message`,
    62→    62→  `attachmentUrl`).
    63→    63→- **`Dispute`** — `bookingId`, `raisedById`, `againstId`, `reason` (`damage`/`late_return`/
    64→    64→  `wrong_item`/`payment`/`other`), `description`, `evidenceUrls` (JSON), `status`
    65→    65→  (`open`/`owner_responded`/`admin_reviewing`/`resolved`/`closed`), `resolution`
    66→    66→  (`refund_customer`/`favor_owner`/`split`/`warning`), `resolutionNote`, `resolvedById`,
    67→    67→  `resolvedAt`.
    68→    68→- **`DisputeMessage`** — discussion thread for a dispute; `isStaff` flag marks admin messages.
    69→    69→
    70→    70→`User` got `trustScore Int @default(50)` + booking/payment/dispute relations; `Equipment` got
    71→    71→`cancellationPolicy String @default("flexible")` and `autoApprove Boolean @default(false)`.
    72→    72→
    73→    73→### Booking state machine (`src/lib/booking-service.ts`)
    74→    74→
    75→    75→```
    76→    76→pending ──▶ held ──▶ confirmed ──▶ picked_up ──▶ in_use ──▶ return_pending ──▶ returned ──▶ inspected ──▶ completed
    77→    77→   │          │          │            │             │             │
    78→    78→   ▼          ▼          ▼            ▼             ▼             ▼
    79→    79→cancelled  cancelled  cancelled   late/        late/         late/         (inspected may go to disputed)
    80→    80→                      cancelled   disputed              disputed
    81→    81→```
    82→    82→
    83→    83→Branch states:
    84→    84→- **`cancelled`** — terminal; the matching `booked` availability block is removed.
    85→    85→- **`late`** — auto-detected by cron when `endDate` passes for any active status; can transition
    86→    86→  back to `returned` (owner confirms late return) or to `disputed`/`cancelled`.
    87→    87→- **`disputed`** — set when a `Dispute` is raised against the booking; resolved back to
    88→    88→  `completed` (admin) or `cancelled` (admin).
    89→    89→
    90→    90→Legal transitions are enforced by `assertTransition(from, to)` which throws
    91→    91→`INVALID_TRANSITION 422` on illegal moves. Every transition writes a `BookingStatusHistory` row.
    92→    92→
    93→    93→### Availability engine
    94→    94→
    95→    95→`checkAvailability(equipmentId, startDate, endDate, excludeBookingId?)` returns
    96→    96→`{ available, conflicts[] }`. Conflicts are checked against three sources:
    97→    97→
    98→    98→1. **Active bookings** — `status in (held, confirmed, picked_up, in_use, return_pending,
    99→    99→   returned, inspected)`.
   100→   100→2. **Availability blocks** — `owner_blocked`, `maintenance`, `booked`.
   101→   101→3. **Maintenance windows**.
   102→   102→
   103→   103→`Equipment.bufferHours` is applied as a buffer around the requested range (subtract from
   104→   104→`startDate`, add to `endDate`) before checking. On booking create, a `booked` availability
   105→   105→block is written atomically with the booking; on cancel/complete the block is removed.
   106→   106→
   107→   107→### Pricing & commission
   108→   108→
   109→   109→`calculateBookingPrice()` reuses the Phase 1 `calculatePrice()` (weekend surcharge, long
   110→   110→discount, seasonal) and adds a flat **10% platform commission** (`commissionAmount`).
   111→   111→`totalAmount` is the rental total (excluding deposit); `depositAmount` is held separately and
   112→   112→released on completion (or refunded in full/partial on cancellation/dispute resolution per
   113→   113→policy).
   114→   114→
   115→   115→### Cancellation policies (`calculateRefund()`)
   116→   116→
   117→   117→| Policy     | More than          | Within               |
   118→   118→| ---------- | ------------------ | -------------------- |
   119→   119→| `flexible` | 24h → **100%**     | 24h → **50%**        |
   120→   120→| `moderate`| 48h → **50%**      | 48h → **25%**        |
   121→   121→| `strict`  | 72h → **25%**      | 72h → **0%**         |
   122→   122→
   123→   123→`completed` and `returned` bookings refund `0`.
   124→   124→
   125→   125→### Checkout hold & cron jobs
   126→   126→
   127→   127→- A new booking enters `held` with `holdExpiresAt = now + 10 min`. If the customer doesn't
   128→   128→  confirm + pay within 10 minutes, the cron job `releaseExpiredHolds()` auto-cancels the
   129→   129→  booking and removes the `booked` block.
   130→   130→- `detectLateReturns()` flips any active booking past `endDate` to `late`.
   131→   131→- Both jobs are exposed at `POST /api/v1/cron/run?job=release-holds|detect-late|all`. There is
   132→   132→  no internal scheduler yet — see Known Issues.

   178→   178→### Intelligence API (6 endpoints)
   179→   179→
   180→   180→| Endpoint                              | Auth            | Description |
   181→   181→| ------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------- |
   182→   182→| `GET /intelligence/pricing-suggestions` | owner / admin | Demand-aware pricing per active listing. Demand score = `(wishlists×2 + reviews×3 + bookings×1) ÷ monthsSinceListed`. Compares current price against category average.    185→   185→| `GET /intelligence/fraud-flags`        | admin           | Rule-based anomaly detection. Customers can only query their own; admins can query anyone. |
   187→   187→| `GET /intelligence/late-prediction`   | owner / admin   | Active bookings with risk score = `trustRisk × 0.4 + timeRisk × 0.6` where trustRisk scales by trust < 50 and timeRisk scales by hours < 24. Returns `predictions[]` with `prediction` (`on_track`/`at_risk`/`likely_late`), `recommendation`, `riskScore`, `hoursRemaining`. Also returns `atRiskCount` and `likelyLateCount`. |
   188→   188→| `POST /intelligence/what-if`          | owner / admin   | Price/availability simulator. Body `{ equipmentId, dailyPrice, availableDaysPerMonth }`. Returns `current` (dailyPrice, estimatedUtilization, monthlyRevenue, annualRevenue), `simulated` (new dailyPrice, availableDaysPerMonth, estimatedUtilization, monthlyRevenue, annualRevenue, sampleBookingTotal), `comparison` (revenueDiff, revenueDiffPct, demandMultiplier). Demand multiplier applies price elasticity (price↑ → demand↓). |

   290→   290→## Known issues / limitations
   291→   291→
   292→   292→### Phase 1
   293→   293→
   294→   294→1. **SQLite has no native geo index.** Distance filtering & `sort=distance` use the Haversine
   295→   295→   formula computed in JS over the candidate set (`src/lib/geo.ts`). Fine for the seeded catalog
   296→   296→   (~30 listings); will revisit with PostGIS or an FTS5 table in a later phase.
   297→   297→2. **No full-text search.** Keyword search uses Prisma `contains` (SQL `LIKE '%q%'`). No
   298→   298→   relevance ranking, no stop-words. A later phase may add SQLite FTS5 or an external index.
   299→   299→3. **Local uploads only.** Files land in `public/uploads/`. The upload interface is
   300→   300→   Cloudinary-pluggable; swapping means re-implementing three functions in `src/lib/upload.ts`.
   301→   301→   No image resizing/optimization is done yet (`sharp` is a dependency but not yet wired).
   302→   302→4. **Upload validation surfaces as 500.** `parseAndSaveImages` throws plain `Error`s with a `.code`
   303→   303→   (`NO_FILES`/`BAD_TYPE`/`TOO_LARGE`/`TOO_MANY`) which currently flow through `handleError` as
   304→   304→   `INTERNAL_ERROR` (500). The API contract documents these as `VALIDATION_ERROR` (400) for client
   305→   305→   semantics; a small `handler.ts` patch will map them explicitly.
   306→   306→5. **Email is dev-console only.** Reset links are printed to the server log; no SMTP in sandbox.
   307→   307→6. **`Equipment.moderatedById` and `AvailabilityBlock.ownerId` / `MaintenanceWindow.ownerId` /
   308→   308→   `MaintenanceLog.ownerId` are denormalized** — not declared as Prisma relations to avoid circular
   309→   309→   cascades; referential integrity is enforced in app code.
   310→   310→7. **Rate limiting is in-memory.** Correct for single-server sandbox; swap to Redis in production
   311→   311→   behind the same `rateLimit()` signature.
   312→   312→8. **`next-auth` is installed but unused.** Custom HS256 JWT was chosen for fine-grained control
   313→   313→   (see `DECISIONS.md §2`). The dependency can be removed in a cleanup pass.
   314→   314→
   315→   315→### Phase 2
   316→   316→
   317→   317→9. **Payments are simulated.** `POST /bookings/:id/payments` writes a `charge` + a `hold`
   318→   318→   `Payment` row with `status='completed'` and a `sim_*` reference. There is no real gateway
   319→   319→   integration; the `Payment` interface is Stripe/Razorpay-pluggable behind the same route shape.
   320→   320→10. **Cron is API-callable but not auto-scheduled.** `POST /cron/run?job=…` works, but an
   321→   321→    external scheduler (e.g. Vercel Cron, node-cron, or a k8s CronJob) needs to invoke it for
   322→   322→    hold-release + late-detection to actually run on a clock.
   323→   323→11. **Socket.IO is server-side only.** The mini socket service runs on port 3003 and emits
   324→   324→    `availability:updated` + booking chat events, but the frontend still polls every 30s.
   325→   325→    Wiring a frontend socket client is a follow-up.
   326→   326→12. **No FullCalendar.** The owner-calendar and equipment-detail availability calendar use a
   327→   327→    custom month-grid component instead of FullCalendar (the library was not installed).
   328→   328→13. **Idempotency keys are optional.** `POST /bookings` accepts an `idempotencyKey` and
   329→   329→    `POST /bookings/:id/payments` synthesises one from the booking id; clients that omit the
   330→   330→    key can still create duplicate bookings on retry.
   331→   331→14. **`Equipment.autoApprove` is not yet surfaced in the listing form.** The booking flow
   332→   332→    honours it (auto-approve → straight to `confirmed`), but there is no UI toggle to set it.
   333→   333→15. **Two-way review.** `POST /bookings/:id/review` allows both customer → owner/equipment and
   334→   334→    owner → customer reviews after `completed`; the existing `POST /reviews` endpoint remains
   335→   335→    for general equipment reviews. Trust score is nudged by ±2 per star above/below 3.
   336→   336→
   337→   337→### Phase 3
   338→   338→
   339→   339→16. **Pricing suggestions are rule-based, not ML.** The demand score is a weighted formula
   340→   340→    (`(wishlists×2 + reviews×3 + bookings×1) ÷ monthsSinceListed`) with three threshold rules
   341→   341→    (raise 15%, lower 10%, align to category). The `reasoning` string is human-readable; an ML
   342→   342→    model could be plugged in behind the same response shape.
   343→   343→17. **Recommendations are co-booking + content-based.** No matrix factorization or embeddings;
   344→   344→    `frequently_together` counts co-occurring bookings by the same customers. Adequate for the
   345→   345→    seeded catalog; revisit with a real model at scale.
   346→   346→18. **Fraud detection is rule-based.** Five rules with fixed thresholds (`≥3` failed payments,
   347→   347→    `≥5` cancellations, `≥3` disputes, trust `<30`, days `≤0`). No ML anomaly model.
   348→   348→19. **Trust score factors are deterministic.** The 7-factor breakdown is computed live; the
   349→   349→    stored `User.trustScore` is updated only on review creation (`±(rating-3)*2`).
   350→   350→20. **Late prediction uses a 40/60 trust/time risk blend.** The weights are hard-coded; tuning
   351→   351→    should be data-driven once real late-return outcomes exist.
   352→   352→21. **What-if uses a price-elasticity heuristic.** `demandMultiplier = max(0.5, 1 - (priceChangeRatio - 1) * 0.3)` for price increases; `1 + (1 - priceChangeRatio) * 0.2` for decreases. Real
   353→   353→    elasticity should be derived from booking history per category.
   354→   354→22. **Utilization caps at 100%.** `utilizationRate = min(100, totalDaysRented ÷ daysSinceListed × 100)`. Multi-booking overlap on the same day is not subtracted.
